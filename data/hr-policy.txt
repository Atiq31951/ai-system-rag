SECTION 1 - DEFINITIONS
================================================================================
1.1  "Working day" means Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays.
1.2  "Weekly holiday" means Friday and Saturday.
1.3  "Office hours" means 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Bangladesh Standard Time (BST).
1.4  "Late mark" means arrival or first check-in after the grace time defined
     in Section 3.
1.5  "Paid leave" means the 25 days of annual paid leave described in
     Section 4. It covers vacation, personal work and casual needs. Paid
     leave is available to permanent employees only.
1.6  "Leave balance" means the paid leave days an employee has accrued and
     not yet used.
1.7  "HR acknowledgement" means written approval recorded in the HR system
     (HRMS) or by official company email from HR. Verbal approval, chat
     messages to a team lead, or social media messages are NOT HR
     acknowledgement.
1.8  "Unauthorized leave" means any absence from work on a working day that
     does not have HR acknowledgement.
1.9  "Daily salary" means monthly gross salary divided by 30.
1.10 "Line manager" means the person an employee directly reports to.
1.11 "Leave year" means 1 January to 31 December.
1.12 "Permanent employee" means an employee who has completed probation and
     has received a written confirmation letter from HR. The date on that
     letter is the "confirmation date".
1.13 "Probationary employee" means an employee who has not yet received a
     confirmation letter, including an employee whose probation has been
     extended.

SECTION 2 - WORKING DAYS AND OFFICE HOURS
================================================================================
2.1  Office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday.
2.2  The working day includes a one-hour lunch and prayer break, normally
     taken between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Teams may stagger the break so that
     at least one person is available.
2.3  Total working time is 8 hours per day including the break, which is
     7 hours of active work, and 35 hours of active work per week.
2.4  Employees must check in and check out using the biometric device at the
     office or the HRMS app when working remotely.
2.5  Leaving before 5:00 PM requires prior approval from the line manager.
     Leaving before 3:00 PM without approval is treated as a half-day of
     unauthorized leave (see Section 5).
2.6  During Ramadan, office hours are 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM with no lunch break,
     unless HR announces different hours.
2.7  No employee will be asked to work more than 8 hours in a day or 48
     hours in a week, except as overtime under Section 8.

SECTION 3 - ATTENDANCE, LATE ARRIVAL AND SALARY DEDUCTION
================================================================================
3.1  Grace time: Employees may check in up to 9:15 AM without penalty.
3.2  Late mark: Check-in after 9:15 AM and up to 10:00 AM counts as one
     late mark.
3.3  Late deduction rule: Every 3 late marks in the same calendar month
     result in a deduction of 1 day's salary.
       - 3 late marks in a month  = 1 day's salary deducted
       - 6 late marks in a month  = 2 days' salary deducted
       - 9 late marks in a month  = 3 days' salary deducted
       - 1 or 2 late marks        = no deduction, but recorded
3.4  Late marks reset to zero on the first day of every calendar month. They
     do NOT carry over to the next month.
3.5  The late salary deduction is taken from salary, NOT from the paid leave
     balance.
3.6  Check-in after 10:00 AM without prior approval counts as a half-day of
     unauthorized leave, not as a late mark.
3.7  Exceptions: A late mark is removed if:
       a) the employee informed the line manager AND HR before 9:15 AM with a
          valid reason (medical emergency, accident, natural disaster), and
          HR accepts the reason in writing; or
       b) the office declares general late attendance due to hartal,
          transport strike, severe flooding or similar city-wide disruption.
3.8  Forgetting to check in counts as a late mark unless the line manager
     confirms the arrival time in HRMS on the same day.
3.9  An employee may ask HR to correct a wrong attendance record within
     7 days. After 7 days the record is final.
3.10 Employees with 3 or more months of 3+ late marks in a 6-month period
     will receive a written warning under Section 14.

SECTION 4 - PAID LEAVE (25 DAYS PER YEAR)
================================================================================
4.1  Eligibility: The 25-day paid leave entitlement and all rules in this
     Section 4 apply ONLY to permanent employees (see definition 1.12).
     Probationary employees do not earn or use paid leave; their leave is
     governed by Section 4A.
4.1A Entitlement: Every permanent employee is entitled to 25 days of paid
     leave per full leave year.
4.2  Accrual: Paid leave is earned month by month, starting from the
     confirmation date. Months spent on probation do not earn paid leave.
       - 2 days are credited at the end of every completed month of service
         after the confirmation date.
         (12 months x 2 days = 24 days)
       - 1 additional "Year-End Leave" day is credited on 1 December to
         employees who are permanent on that date.
       - Total for a full year as a permanent employee = 25 days.
       - In the year of confirmation, the employee receives a partial
         entitlement (see Example 15.7).
4.3  Employees may only use leave that has already been credited. Taking
     leave before it is earned ("advance leave") requires HR approval and is
     limited to 3 days.
4.4  Paid leave may be taken as full days or half days (morning half
     9:00 AM to 1:00 PM, afternoon half 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM).
4.5  Weekly holidays and public holidays falling inside a leave period are
     NOT counted as leave days. Example: leave from Thursday to Sunday uses
     2 days (Thursday and Sunday), not 4.
4.6  Carry forward: Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward into the
     next leave year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December.
4.7  Encashment: At the end of the leave year, up to 5 unused days (out of
     the lapsing balance) may be paid out at the daily basic salary rate if
     the employee requests it by 15 December.
4.8  On resignation or termination, the remaining credited leave balance is
     paid out at the daily gross salary rate in the final settlement.
4.9  Employees on probation do not earn paid leave. Any casual or sick
     leave balance left from probation (Section 4A) expires on the
     confirmation date and is not converted into paid leave.
4.10 Unauthorized leave (Section 5) is deducted from this 25-day balance.

================================================================================
SECTION 4A - LEAVE DURING PROBATION
================================================================================
4A.1 Probationary employees are NOT entitled to the 25-day paid leave, carry
     forward, encashment or the Year-End Leave day described in Section 4.
4A.2 Instead, during the probation period (normally 6 months), a
     probationary employee may take:
       - Casual leave : up to 5 days with full pay, for urgent personal
                        needs. Maximum 2 consecutive days at a time.
       - Sick leave   : up to 7 days with full pay, under the same medical
                        certificate rule as Section 6.1.
4A.3 If probation is extended (Section 10.1), the employee receives
     1 additional casual leave day and 1 additional sick leave day for each
     extra month of probation.
4A.4 Casual and sick leave during probation still require HR
     acknowledgement under Section 5.
4A.5 Any absence beyond the casual and sick leave allowed in 4A.2 is leave
     without pay, and 1 day's salary is deducted for each day.
4A.6 Unused probation casual or sick leave is not paid out on confirmation,
     resignation or termination.
4A.7 Attendance and leave records during probation are considered in the
     confirmation decision.
4A.8 The late arrival rules in Section 3 apply to probationary employees in
     the same way as permanent employees.

SECTION 5 - LEAVE APPROVAL PROCESS AND UNAUTHORIZED LEAVE
================================================================================
5.1  Core rule: No employee may take leave without HR acknowledgement. Line
     manager approval alone is not enough; HR must acknowledge the leave in
     HRMS.
5.2  How to apply:
       Step 1 - Submit a leave request in HRMS with dates and reason.
       Step 2 - Line manager approves or rejects within 1 working day.
       Step 3 - HR checks the leave balance and acknowledges within
                1 working day.
       Step 4 - The leave is valid only after Step 3 is complete.
5.3  Notice required:
       - 1 to 2 days of leave : apply at least 3 working days before
       - 3 to 5 days of leave : apply at least 7 working days before
       - 6 or more days       : apply at least 15 working days before
       - Leave adjoining Eid or Durga Puja holidays: apply at least
         30 days before, as approvals are limited per team.
5.4  Emergency leave: If an emergency makes advance application impossible,
     the employee must inform both the line manager and HR (phone or email)
     within 2 hours of office start time, and submit the HRMS request within
     2 working days after returning. If HR accepts the emergency, the leave
     is treated as normal approved leave.
5.5  Unauthorized leave rule: If an employee is absent without HR
     acknowledgement:
       a) For a permanent employee, each day of absence is deducted from
          the 25-day paid leave balance.
       b) For a probationary employee, each day of absence is treated as
          leave without pay and 1 day's salary is deducted. It is NOT taken
          from probation casual or sick leave.
       c) In both cases, the employee receives a written record of
          unauthorized absence.
5.6  If a permanent employee's paid leave balance is zero, each day of unauthorized leave is
     treated as leave without pay and 1 day's salary is deducted for each
     day.
5.7  Repeated unauthorized leave:
       - 1st occurrence in a leave year : recorded, verbal counselling
       - 2nd occurrence                 : written warning
       - 3rd occurrence                 : final written warning
       - Further occurrences            : disciplinary action under
                                          Section 14
5.8  Continuous absence without permission for more than 10 days will be
     handled according to the Bangladesh Labour Act: the company will send a
     written notice asking the employee to explain and return, and the
     employee may lose employment if they do not respond within the legal
     time.
5.9  HR may refuse or reschedule leave during critical project periods, but
     must give a written reason and offer an alternative date.

SECTION 6 - OTHER TYPES OF LEAVE
================================================================================
These leaves are SEPARATE from the 25-day paid leave and do not reduce it.
Unless a rule below says otherwise, Section 6 applies to permanent
employees. Probationary employees use the sick leave in Section 4A instead
of 6.1. Maternity (6.2) and bereavement (6.4) leave apply to all employees
who meet their conditions.

6.1  Sick leave: 14 days per year with full pay.
       - Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical
         certificate from a registered doctor.
       - Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.
       - Sick leave must still be reported to HR on the same day, using the
         emergency process in 5.4.
6.2  Maternity leave: 16 weeks (112 days) with full pay, normally 8 weeks
     before and 8 weeks after the expected delivery date, for employees with
     at least 6 months of service, as required by law.
6.3  Paternity leave: 10 working days with full pay, to be taken within
     8 weeks of the child's birth. This is a company benefit.
6.4  Bereavement leave: 3 working days with full pay on the death of a
     spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law.
6.5  Marriage leave: 5 working days with full pay, once during employment.
6.6  Hajj or pilgrimage leave: up to 30 days without pay, once during
     employment, for employees with at least 2 years of service.
6.7  Exam leave: up to 5 days per year without pay for job-related
     professional exams.
6.8  Leave without pay: up to 30 days per year, only with HR and department
     head approval, after the paid leave balance is used.
6.9  All of these leaves also require HR acknowledgement as in Section 5.

SECTION 7 - GOVERNMENT PUBLIC HOLIDAYS OF BANGLADESH
================================================================================
7.1  The company observes all public holidays declared by the Government of
     Bangladesh in the annual holiday gazette issued by the Ministry of
     Public Administration. This includes:
       a) General holidays, and
       b) Holidays declared under executive order.
7.2  Public holidays are fully paid and are NOT deducted from paid leave.
7.3  Holidays that depend on the sighting of the moon (Eid ul Fitr,
     Eid ul Azha, Shab-e-Barat, Shab-e-Qadr, Ashura, Eid-e-Miladunnabi) may
     move by one day. The company follows the date finally announced by the
     government.
7.4  If the government declares an additional holiday during the year
     (for example an extra Durga Puja or Eid day), the company will observe
     it and HR will announce it by email.
7.5  If a public holiday falls on a Friday or Saturday, no substitute
     holiday is given.
7.6  Employees of all religions receive all public holidays. In addition,
     each employee may take up to 2 optional religious or cultural holidays
     per year with 7 days' notice. Permanent employees use paid leave for
     these; probationary employees use probation casual leave (4A.2).
7.7  The holiday list for the next year will be published by HR within
     7 days after the government gazette is released (usually October to
     November). Until then, the current year's pattern is used for planning.
7.8  The full official list for 2026 is in Appendix A.

SECTION 8 - WORKING ON HOLIDAYS, OVERTIME AND COMPENSATORY DAYS
================================================================================
8.1  Work on a weekly holiday or public holiday is only allowed with written
     approval from the department head and HR.
8.2  For each weekly holiday worked, the employee gets 1 compensatory day off,
     to be used within 30 days.
8.3  For each public (festival) holiday worked, the employee gets
     2 compensatory days off with full pay plus 1 additional day's salary.
8.4  Compensatory days do not count toward the 25-day paid leave balance and
     cannot be carried forward past 30 days.
8.5  Overtime: Work beyond 8 hours a day must be approved by the line
     manager in advance. Overtime is paid at twice the hourly basic salary
     for employees covered by the Bangladesh Labour Act. Managerial staff
     receive compensatory time instead.
8.6  No employee may work more than 10 hours in a single day, including
     overtime.

SECTION 9 - REMOTE WORK
================================================================================
9.1  After probation, employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with
     line manager approval.
9.2  Remote work days follow the same office hours (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM) and
     the same late rules (Section 3), using HRMS app check-in.
9.3  Remote employees must be reachable on the company chat tool and attend
     scheduled meetings with camera on when asked.
9.4  Remote work is not leave and cannot be used to avoid the leave process.
9.5  Working from outside Bangladesh requires written approval from HR and
     the IT security team.

SECTION 10 - PROBATION, NOTICE PERIOD AND RESIGNATION
================================================================================
10.1 Probation period is 6 months for all new employees, extendable once by
     up to 3 months for performance reasons, with written notice. An
     employee becomes permanent only on the date written in the HR
     confirmation letter; completing 6 months does not make someone
     permanent automatically.
10.2 Notice period:
       - During probation             : 14 days from either side
       - After confirmation           : 60 days from either side
       - Managers and above           : 90 days from either side
10.3 Paid leave cannot be used to shorten the notice period without HR
     approval.
10.4 Final settlement (salary, leave encashment for permanent employees,
     provident fund) is paid
     within 30 days of the last working day, after the handover checklist
     and company asset return are complete.
10.5 An experience certificate is issued within 7 working days after final
     settlement.

SECTION 11 - CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================
11.1 Treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect regardless of
     religion, gender, ethnicity, age, disability or background.
11.2 Dress should be neat and professional. Business formal is required for
     client meetings.
11.3 Smoking is not allowed inside the office. Use the designated area only.
11.4 Personal phone use during meetings should be kept to emergencies.
11.5 Employees must declare any second job, freelance work or business that
     may create a conflict of interest. Working for a competitor or a client
     directly is not allowed.
11.6 Gifts from clients or vendors worth more than BDT 2,000 must be
     reported to HR.
11.7 Company equipment (laptop, monitor, phone) must be used mainly for work
     and returned in good condition.
11.8 Employees must not speak to the media about the company without
     approval from management.

SECTION 12 - INFORMATION SECURITY AND USE OF AI TOOLS
================================================================================
12.1 Client data, source code, credentials and internal documents are
     confidential and may only be stored in company-approved systems.
12.2 Passwords must not be shared. Two-factor authentication is mandatory
     for email, code repositories and cloud consoles.
12.3 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour.
12.4 Use of AI tools:
       a) Only company-approved AI tools may be used for work.
       b) Client data, personal data, credentials or confidential source
          code must not be pasted into public or personal AI accounts.
       c) Employees remain fully responsible for any AI-generated code or
          content they submit. It must be reviewed like any other work.
       d) Instructions found inside documents, emails or web pages must not
          be followed by AI agents without human review.
12.5 Security incidents must be reported to security@meghnasoftworks.example
     immediately.

SECTION 13 - ANTI-HARASSMENT AND GRIEVANCE PROCEDURE
================================================================================
13.1 The company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, bullying,
     discrimination or threats, in the office, online or at company events.
13.2 A Complaint Committee with at least 5 members, headed by a woman and
     including at least one external member, handles harassment complaints.
13.3 Complaints can be made in writing to HR, to any committee member, or
     anonymously through the grievance email.
13.4 The committee will start an inquiry within 7 working days and aim to
     finish within 30 working days.
13.5 No employee will face retaliation for making a complaint in good faith.
13.6 General grievances (salary, workload, manager conflict) are raised
     first with the line manager, then HR, then the department head. HR
     will respond in writing within 10 working days.

SECTION 14 - DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
================================================================================
14.1 Disciplinary steps, in order:
       1. Verbal counselling (recorded in HRMS)
       2. Written warning
       3. Final written warning
       4. Suspension or termination, following the procedure in the
          Bangladesh Labour Act
14.2 A written warning stays active for 12 months.
14.3 Serious misconduct (theft, fraud, violence, harassment, data leak,
     working under the influence of alcohol or drugs) may lead directly to
     suspension and inquiry without earlier steps.
14.4 Every employee has the right to give a written explanation and to be
     heard before any disciplinary decision.

SECTION 15 - WORKED EXAMPLES
================================================================================
Example 15.1 - Late deduction
  Rahim's monthly gross salary is BDT 60,000. His daily salary is
  60,000 / 30 = BDT 2,000.
  In March he checked in at 9:20, 9:40, 9:25 and 9:50 (4 late marks).
  4 late marks = one full group of 3. Deduction = 1 day = BDT 2,000.
  The 4th late mark does not carry to April.

Example 15.2 - Grace time
  Nusrat checked in at 9:14 AM on 5 days in a month.
  9:14 is within grace time. Late marks = 0. Deduction = 0.

Example 15.3 - Arrival after 10:00 AM
  Karim arrived at 10:30 AM without prior approval.
  This is a half-day of unauthorized leave, not a late mark.
  0.5 day is deducted from his paid leave balance.

Example 15.4 - Leave without HR acknowledgement
  Tania's manager said "okay" on chat for 2 days off, but she never
  submitted a request in HRMS. She took the 2 days.
  Result: 2 days of unauthorized leave. 2 days are deducted from her 25-day
  balance, and an unauthorized absence record is created.

Example 15.5 - Unauthorized leave with zero balance
  Sabbir has 0 paid leave days left and is absent 1 day without HR
  acknowledgement. Result: leave without pay, 1 day's salary deducted.

Example 15.6 - Leave around weekends
  Mitu takes leave from Thursday to the next Sunday.
  Friday and Saturday are weekly holidays and are not counted.
  Leave used = 2 days (Thursday + Sunday).

Example 15.7 - Accrual in the year of confirmation
  Farhan joined on 1 January and was on probation until 30 June. He earned
  no paid leave from January to June. During probation he could take up to
  5 days casual leave and 7 days sick leave.
  He was confirmed as permanent on 1 July. From July to December he earns
  6 x 2 = 12 days. On 1 December he is permanent, so he also gets the
  Year-End Leave day. Paid leave for this year = 13 days.
  From the next year he earns the full 25 days.

Example 15.8 - Mid-year joiner still on probation
  Lamia joined on 1 August. She is on probation until 31 January next year.
  She earns no paid leave this year and does not get the Year-End Leave
  day, because she is not permanent on 1 December.
  She can take up to 5 days casual leave and 7 days sick leave during her
  probation. If she is confirmed on 1 February, she starts earning 2 days
  per month from February.

Example 15.8A - Unauthorized leave during probation
  Rafi is on probation and was absent for 1 day without HR
  acknowledgement. He has 3 casual leave days left, but they are not used.
  The day is leave without pay: 1 day's salary is deducted, and the
  unauthorized absence is recorded for his confirmation review.

Example 15.9 - Working on a public holiday
  Arif worked on Victory Day (16 December) with approval.
  He gets 2 compensatory days off and 1 additional day's salary.

SECTION 16 - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
================================================================================
Q1: How many paid leave days do I get in a year?
A1: Permanent employees get 25 days: 2 days per completed month (24 days)
    plus 1 Year-End Leave day on 1 December. Probationary employees do not
    get the 25-day paid leave (see Q19).

Q2: Is sick leave included in the 25 days?
A2: No. Sick leave is separate: 14 days per year with full pay.

Q3: What time does office start and end?
A3: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday, with a one-hour break.

Q4: What happens if I come late?
A4: Up to 9:15 AM is grace time. After 9:15 AM it is a late mark. Every
    3 late marks in a calendar month cuts 1 day's salary.

Q5: Is the late penalty taken from my leave?
A5: No. Late deductions come from salary. Only unauthorized absences are
    deducted from the leave balance.

Q6: My manager approved my leave on chat. Is that enough?
A6: No. Leave is valid only after HR acknowledges it in HRMS. Otherwise it
    is unauthorized leave. For permanent employees it is deducted from the
    25-day balance; for probationary employees it is leave without pay.

Q7: Are government holidays deducted from my leave?
A7: No. All government public holidays are paid and are not counted as
    leave.

Q8: Which days are weekly holidays?
A8: Friday and Saturday.

Q9: Can I carry forward unused leave?
A9: Yes, up to 10 days. Days above 10 lapse on 31 December, but up to 5 of
    the lapsing days can be paid out if you request by 15 December.

Q10: If a public holiday falls on a Friday, do I get another day off?
A10: No substitute holiday is given for holidays falling on weekly holidays.

Q11: How many days are the Eid holidays in 2026?
A11: Eid ul Fitr is 5 days (19 to 23 March) and Eid ul Azha is 6 days
     (26 to 31 May), as per the government list. Moon-dependent dates may
     shift.

Q12: What if I have an emergency and cannot apply in advance?
A12: Inform your line manager and HR within 2 hours of office start time,
     then submit the HRMS request within 2 working days after returning.

Q13: How much notice do I need for 6 or more days of leave?
A13: At least 15 working days. For leave next to Eid or Durga Puja, at
     least 30 days.

Q14: Do late marks carry over to the next month?
A14: No. They reset on the first day of every month.

Q15: Can I use AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude at work?
A15: Only company-approved AI tools, and never with client data, personal
     data, credentials or confidential source code in personal accounts.

Q16: How long is the notice period after confirmation?
A16: 60 days, or 90 days for managers and above.

Q17: What happens if I work on a public holiday?
A17: With approval, you get 2 compensatory days off plus 1 extra day's
     salary.

Q18: How long is maternity leave?
A18: 16 weeks with full pay, for employees with at least 6 months of
     service.

Q19: I am on probation. What leave can I take?
A19: Up to 5 days casual leave and 7 days sick leave with full pay during
     the 6-month probation. You do not earn the 25-day paid leave until you
     are confirmed as permanent.

Q20: Does my paid leave start after 6 months automatically?
A20: No. It starts from the confirmation date on your HR confirmation
     letter. If your probation is extended, paid leave starts later.

Q21: Do my probation months count toward the 25 days after I am confirmed?
A21: No. Paid leave is earned only for months completed after the
     confirmation date. Unused probation casual or sick leave is not carried
     over.

Q22: Do late arrival rules apply during probation?
A22: Yes. The 9:15 AM grace time and the 3 late marks = 1 day's salary
     rule apply to everyone, including probationary employees.
