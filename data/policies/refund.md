# Refund Policy

## Who Can Get a Refund
A refund is available in four cases: you returned an item within the return window, you cancelled an order before it was dispatched, you received a damaged or defective item, or you received a fake (counterfeit) item. You must request the refund within 14 days of receiving the item. For cancelled orders, the refund starts automatically and no request is needed.

## Items That Cannot Be Refunded
Some items cannot be refunded: digital gift cards, items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance", and personal hygiene products (such as shampoo, cosmetics or skincare) that have been opened or used. Items returned after the 14-day window are also not eligible for a refund. These limits do not apply if the item was fake, damaged or defective when it arrived.

## Refunds for Fake, Damaged or Defective Items
If you receive a fake, damaged or defective item, you are always eligible for a full refund, even if it is a hygiene product that you have opened. Report the problem within 7 days of delivery and include clear photos of the product, the packaging and the label. In these cases we also refund the original shipping fee, and you do not pay any return shipping cost.

## How to Request a Refund
To request a refund, contact customer support through chat with your order ID and the reason for the refund. For damaged, defective or fake items, attach photos when asked. Support will confirm whether you need to send the item back and will share the return instructions. Do not send items back before support confirms, because unapproved returns may be delayed.

## Inspection and Approval
After we receive a returned item, our inspection team checks it within 2 business days. If the item meets the refund conditions, the refund is approved and you are notified by email. If the refund is rejected, we explain the reason and send the item back to you at no cost.

## Refund Processing Time
After approval, we process the refund within 7 to 10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may take an additional 3 to 5 business days to show the money on your statement. In total, most customers see their refund within 10 to 15 business days after approval.

## Refund Method for Card and Online Payments
If you paid by credit card, debit card or online wallet, the refund goes back to the same payment method you used. We cannot send the refund to a different card or account. If that card is closed or expired, contact your bank, and it will forward the money to you.

## Refund Method for Cash on Delivery Orders
If you paid by Cash on Delivery (COD), we cannot refund in physical cash. You can choose a store credit voucher, which arrives within 24 hours, or a bank transfer, which arrives within 5 to 7 business days after approval. For a bank transfer, you must provide the account holder name, bank account number and branch routing code.

## Partial Refunds
A partial refund is given when only some items in an order are returned, or when a returned item shows damage or missing parts that were not there at delivery. Support explains the exact amount and reason before the partial refund is processed.

## Shipping and Extra Fees in Refunds
Original shipping fees and gift wrapping charges are not refunded, except when the refund is caused by our mistake, such as a wrong, damaged, defective or fake item. For normal returns where you changed your mind, the customer pays the return shipping cost.
