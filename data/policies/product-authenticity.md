# Product Authenticity and Quality Guarantee

## Our Genuine Product Guarantee
ShopEase guarantees that every product sold on our website is 100% genuine and original. All items are sourced directly from the brand owner, the manufacturer, or an authorized distributor. We never knowingly sell counterfeit, replica, imitation, or unauthorized goods. Any seller or supplier found selling fake products is permanently removed from our platform.

## What Counts as a Fake, Damaged, or Defective Product
A product is considered fake (counterfeit) if it is not made by the brand shown on the label, even if the packaging looks original. Common signs include a wrong or missing barcode, spelling mistakes on the label, a different smell, color, texture, or consistency than usual, a broken or missing seal, or a missing batch number or expiry date. A product is damaged if it arrives broken, leaking, crushed, or with torn packaging. A product is defective if it does not work as it should, for example an electronic item that does not turn on.

## Personal Care and Cosmetic Products
For personal care items such as shampoo, skincare, perfume, and cosmetics, customers can report a suspected fake product even after opening it. If the product looks, smells, or feels clearly different from the original brand product, stop using it immediately and keep the bottle or container with the remaining product. Do not throw it away, because our quality team may need it for verification. Opened personal care products cannot be returned for change of mind, only for authenticity, damage, or quality problems.

## How to Report a Problem (Within 7 Days)
You must report a fake, damaged, or defective product within 7 calendar days from the delivery date. To report, contact ShopEase customer support through the chat, share your order ID, and describe the problem. Reports made after 7 days are not covered by this guarantee, but you can still contact the brand for manufacturer warranty support where available.

## Proof You Need to Provide
To process your claim, please send clear photos or a short video showing the product from all sides, the barcode and batch number, the expiry date if any, the outer box and shipping label, and the specific problem you found. For fake product claims, a photo comparing it with an original product or the brand's official images is helpful but not required. Claims without proof cannot be processed.

## Verification Process (Within 48 Hours)
After we receive your proof, our quality assurance team reviews the claim within 48 hours. If needed, we may contact you for more details or arrange a free pickup of the product for physical inspection. You will be informed of the result through chat or email.

## Your Options After Verification
If your claim is verified, you can choose either a full refund or a free replacement of the same product. A full refund includes the product price and the original shipping fee, and is sent to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval. A replacement is shipped free of charge as soon as the item is in stock. If the item is out of stock, you will receive a full refund instead.

## Return Shipping and Fees
For verified fake, damaged, or defective products, you pay no return shipping fee and no restocking fee. ShopEase arranges a free pickup from your address if the item needs to be returned. Please keep the product and all original packaging until your claim is closed.

## What Is Not Covered
This guarantee does not cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, wrong installation, or normal wear and tear after use. It also does not cover change of mind, wrong size or color chosen by the customer, or minor packaging differences that brands sometimes make between production batches. For change-of-mind returns, please see our Return Policy.

## Manufacturer Warranty
Many electronic and branded products also come with an official manufacturer warranty, usually between 6 months and 2 years depending on the product. This warranty is provided by the brand, not by ShopEase. To claim it, keep the warranty card and the original invoice from your package, and contact the brand's authorized service center. ShopEase customer support can help you find the right service center if needed.
