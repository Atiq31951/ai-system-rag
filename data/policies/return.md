# Return Policy

## Return Window
Customers can request a return within 14 calendar days from the delivery date shown in their order tracking. Return requests made after 14 days are not accepted, except for products that arrive damaged, defective or fake. Orders that are still pending or shipped cannot be returned; a pending order can be cancelled instead.

## How to Request a Return
To request a return, open the order in the account dashboard and choose "Request Return", or contact customer support with the order ID (for example, ord-101). The customer must give the reason for the return and, for damaged or defective items, at least one clear photo. Support reviews every return request within 1 business day and replies with an approval or a rejection reason.

## Items That Can Be Returned
Most products can be returned, including electronics, accessories, footwear, apparel and furniture. Electronics can be returned only if all parts, cables and manuals are included and the device is not damaged by the customer. Furniture must be returned unassembled or in its original assembled state, without stains or scratches.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned
Opened personal care and hygiene products, underwear, socks, gift cards and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned. These items can only be returned if they arrive damaged, defective, wrong or fake. Products damaged by the customer after delivery cannot be returned.

## Condition of Returned Items
Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in the same condition as when they were delivered. All original tags, seals, packaging, manuals and accessories must be included. If a returned item shows signs of use, damage caused by the customer or missing parts, the return is rejected and the item is sent back to the customer at no refund.

## Return Shipping Cost
If the item is damaged, defective, wrong or fake, return shipping is free. If the customer returns an item because they changed their mind or chose the wrong size or color, a return shipping fee of $5.00 is deducted from the refund. For furniture returned for change of mind, the return shipping fee is $20.00.

## Pickup and Drop-off
After a return is approved, the customer can choose a free courier pickup or drop the package at any FastShip point. Courier pickup happens within 2 to 3 business days after approval. The item must be packed securely, preferably in the original box, with the order ID written on the package.

## Inspection After Return
When the returned item reaches our warehouse, it is inspected within 2 business days. If the item passes inspection, the refund is started on the same day. If it fails inspection, the customer receives an email with the reason and photos.

## Refund After a Return
Refunds for returned items follow the Refund Policy. The refund is sent to the original payment method, minus any return shipping fee that applies. The customer receives a confirmation message when the refund is started.

## Exchanges
Direct exchanges are offered only when an item arrives damaged, defective or wrong; in that case, the same product is sent again at no cost, if it is in stock. For a different size, color or model, the customer must return the original item for a refund and place a new order. If the replacement product is out of stock, the customer receives a full refund instead.

## Damaged, Wrong or Fake Items on Arrival
If a product arrives damaged, defective, wrong or fake, the customer must report it within 7 days of delivery with photos. These cases always get free return shipping and the choice of a replacement or a full refund. For more details about fake or counterfeit products, see the Product Authenticity Policy.
