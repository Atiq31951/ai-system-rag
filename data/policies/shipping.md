# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Coverage
ShopEase ships to all addresses within Malaysia, including East Malaysia (Sabah and Sarawak). We do not offer international shipping at this time. All orders are shipped from our main warehouse in Penang through our delivery partner, FastShip.

## Order Processing Time
Orders placed before 2:00 PM (Malaysia time) on a business day are packed and handed to the courier on the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM, on weekends, or on public holidays are processed on the next business day. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Malaysian public holidays.

## Standard Delivery Time
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days to major cities such as Kuala Lumpur, Penang, Johor Bahru and Ipoh. Delivery to other areas in West Malaysia takes 5 to 7 business days. Delivery to East Malaysia (Sabah and Sarawak) takes 7 to 10 business days. Delivery time is counted from the day the order is handed to the courier, not from the day the order is placed.

## Express Delivery Time
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available only for major cities in West Malaysia. Express delivery can be selected at checkout and cannot be added after the order is placed. Express delivery is not available for East Malaysia.

## Shipping Costs
Standard delivery is free for orders with a total of $50.00 or more. Orders below $50.00 have a flat standard shipping fee of $4.99. Express delivery costs a flat $9.99 for any order total. Shipping fees are shown at checkout before payment.

## Order Tracking
When an order is handed to the courier, the customer receives a tracking number by email. The customer can track the order on the FastShip website or by asking the ShopEase support assistant. Tracking updates usually appear within 12 to 24 hours after the courier picks up the package. If there is no tracking update for more than 2 business days, the customer should contact support.

## Changing the Delivery Address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is pending, before it is handed to the courier. Once an order is shipped, the address cannot be changed by ShopEase. For shipped orders, the customer must contact the courier with the tracking number to request a change.

## Order On Hold Because of an Address Problem
If the courier marks a package as on hold because the address or postal code is invalid or incomplete, the customer must confirm the correct address within 3 business days. The customer can confirm the address by contacting ShopEase support or the courier. If the address is not confirmed within 3 business days, the package is returned to our warehouse, and the order is cancelled with a refund of the product price. The original shipping fee is not refunded in this case.

## Failed Delivery Attempts
The courier makes up to 2 delivery attempts. If no one is available to receive the package, the courier leaves a notice and the package stays at the nearest courier hub for 5 business days. If the package is not collected within 5 business days, it is returned to our warehouse and the order is cancelled with a refund of the product price, excluding shipping fees.

## Delayed Orders
An order is considered delayed if it has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date. Customers with a delayed order should contact support with their order ID, and we will open an investigation with the courier within 1 business day. Delays caused by public holidays, severe weather or courier disruptions are communicated by email when possible.

## Lost Packages
If a delayed order has no tracking update for 7 business days, it is treated as lost. For lost packages, the customer can choose a free replacement (if the product is in stock) or a full refund, including the shipping fee. The customer does not need to wait for the courier's investigation to finish to receive the replacement or refund.

## Damaged Packages
If a package arrives visibly damaged, the customer should take photos of the package and the product and report it to support within 48 hours of delivery. After review, ShopEase offers a free replacement or a full refund for damaged products. The customer does not need to pay any shipping fee to return a damaged product.
