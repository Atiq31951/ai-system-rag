# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. We also accept local online banking, digital wallets such as bKash and Nagad, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All online card transactions are processed securely through 256-bit SSL encrypted payment gateways.

## Cash on Delivery Conditions
Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to a maximum total value of $100.00. Orders exceeding $100.00 must be prepaid using an online payment method before processing. The exact cash amount must be handed to the delivery courier upon arrival, as couriers may not carry exact change.

## Payment Failure and Billing
If your online payment fails during checkout, the order will remain in a pending state for up to 30 minutes before automatic cancellation. If your bank account was debited despite a checkout failure, the deducted amount will automatically be reversed by your bank within 24 to 48 hours.

## Currency and Pricing
All prices listed on the store website are inclusive of applicable sales taxes and displayed in USD. Your bank or card issuer may apply foreign exchange conversion fees if you pay using a card issued outside the domestic billing region.
